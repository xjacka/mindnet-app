Privacy policy

Effective 9 October 2026 · public beta

In short: we store your account and what you create in MindNet. We do not store the text of web articles after processing them. Articles are processed by a language model at the providers listed below. We sell nothing and show no ads. You delete your account with all its content yourself in the app's settings.

Who processes the data

The controller is Lukáš Janeček, who runs MindNet. Contact for anything about your data: xjacka@gmail.com.

What data and why

DataPurpose
Account — email, name, handle, profile picture (from Google or your own), language and app settings Signing in, showing your profile to people you allow, and posts in your language.
What you send — links to articles, pasted text and files (PDF, EPUB), selected passages, comments, bookmarks, questions about articles, followed sites and channels Posts are made from it. Pasted text and files are stored because there is no other original.
What is created — posts, translations, summaries and their numeric fingerprints (embeddings) Feed, search and recommending similar content.
How you use MindNet — stars, saved, read and hidden posts, interests from the initial setup Ordering the feed and choosing what interests you. These signals also produce a short written description of your interests that the model receives when choosing content for you.
People and groups — whom you follow, who follows you, groups, shares and public links The social part: what to show to whom. A post is visible only to those you make it available to.
Device — notification token Sending notifications to your phone.
Own agent keys Connecting your own agent, if you use one.

The text of web articles is not stored. The server fetches an article, processes it and keeps only what came out of it (posts, a short extract, the title). The exception is the text of a page the server cannot fetch and your device sends instead: it is kept for at most 30 days.

The legal basis is the performance of a contract — the service does not work without this data — and, for improving the service, legitimate interest.

We do not sell data and do not use it for advertising.

How long

  • Your account and content: until you delete the account.
  • Untouched posts from followed sources and never-opened articles in the reading list: 30 days. The periods are listed in the app's settings under Data retention.
  • The shared cache of processed articles (extracts, translations) does not belong to any one account; whatever nothing points to any more is removed by a daily clean-up.

Your rights

You have the right to access your data, have it corrected or erased, restrict processing, data portability and the right to object. You delete your account yourself in the app's settings — the profile and everything attached to it is deleted, including posts you shared with others. For anything else write to xjacka@gmail.com. You can lodge a complaint with the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection (uoou.gov.cz) or the supervisory authority where you live.

Browser extension

The extension reads a page only when you open it or use it from the right-click menu, and only the page you are on. It sends the title, address, selected passage and possibly the page text — nothing in the background. It keeps your sign-in in the browser's storage and never stores your password.

Changes

When this policy changes, we update the date at the top and announce significant changes in the app.
